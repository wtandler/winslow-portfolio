## The Problem

Program intake required 6-10 hours of manual work to prepare a charter, assess readiness and risk, and decide where the program should route. The portfolio office handles about 60 programs each quarter, so the same research and document preparation repeated at scale. Almost all of that time went to gathering context and formatting documents, and very little went to the routing decision itself.

## What I Noticed

Nobody had asked for this product. The portfolio office treated the 6-10 hours as fixed, and the only requests were for more reviewers. Working with the portfolio stakeholders, I saw that most of a reviewer's time went to reading and preparing documents, which AI can do, and little went to the decision, which should stay with a person.

## The Product Decision

I proposed a workspace in which AI prepares the material and a person makes the decision. Six specialist agents assemble source context, draft the charter, complete the readiness and risk review, and recommend routing. Each output carries source links, confidence indicators, version history, validation checks, and a human approval step. I considered a simpler option, an intake form with an AI summary. It would have saved minutes per program instead of hours, because the reviewer would still have done the research.

## What I Owned

I identified the opportunity with portfolio stakeholders, defined the product, designed the agent workflow and human controls, directed the build, launched it, and continue to own support, governance, compliance, and product changes.

## What Shipped

A user brings an outcome and supporting documents. The workspace returns a draft program charter, a readiness and risk review, and a routing recommendation, each traceable to its sources. The reviewer edits or accepts, and nothing is submitted without human approval. Hard cases arrive flagged, so reviewer attention goes where it is needed.

## Results

The combined process fell from 6-10 hours to about 10 minutes per program, with human approval before submission. The product is live as a v1 with all core functionality in production. It has been demonstrated to the VP leadership group, and rollout follow-ups are scheduled across 4 divisions.

## Microsoft Ecosystem Fit

The six agents run on Dataverse and Microsoft AI services, under the organization's existing sign-in, data, and security controls. The product reads the systems the portfolio office already uses, so it adds no new data store.
